# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to help. Argue about
the code, never about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached. "This breaks on Intel Macs
  because X" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Assume good faith. Most bad pull requests are someone learning, not someone
  wasting your time.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. Open Loom stays deliberately narrow, and
  a rejected feature is not a rejected person.
- Respect that people here work in different languages, timezones and levels of
  experience.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery,
deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private information, or sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to jayden@mortaihq.com. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may delete comments, close issues, or block accounts that breach
this document. Serious or repeated breaches get a permanent ban without a
warning cycle. Maintainers who breach it are held to the same standard as
everyone else.
